Transport Check & Alternatives 交通核实与建议

✓ Transport note: The Airport MRT Express from Taoyuan T2 to Taipei Main Station takes about 35–40 minutes and runs roughly every 15–20 min in the evening, so the 19:00→19:30 slot is realistic if you clear immigration quickly. Buy an EasyCard on arrival — it works on MRT, buses and most convenience stores.
✓ Transport note: Nanjing Fuxing (Songshan–Xindian Line, G) to Zhongshan/Ningxia Market area is only 1–2 stops (~6–8 min by train), so the listed 15 minutes comfortably covers walking + transfer time; Bus 306 is a fine backup if you prefer to stay above ground.
⚠ Route check: If your flight lands later than expected, the Airport MRT's last express train from the airport is around 23:00 — for a late arrival, the non-express (all-stop) Airport MRT or a taxi (~NT$1,000–1,200, 45–60 min) are the safer alternatives to reach Taipei Main Station.

Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Taoyuan International Airport桃园机场

History & Background

Opened in 1979 as Chiang Kai-shek International Airport and renamed Taoyuan International Airport in 2006, this is Taiwan's main international gateway, connecting the island to the world and handling the bulk of its long-haul traffic.

Highlights — What to See
  • Terminal 2's distinctive glass-and-steel roofline
  • Airport MRT check-in counters for early bag-drop
  • Duty-free shopping if you have time before heading into the city

简介与历史

桃园机场于1979年启用，原名中正国际机场，2006年更名为桃园国际机场，是台湾最主要的国际门户机场。

看点推荐

  • 航站楼二的玻璃钢构屋顶设计
  • 可提前在机捷办理登机与行李托运
  • 转机或候车时可逛免税店

Taipei Main Station台北车站

History & Background

The central hub of Taipei since the railway first arrived in 1891 under Qing rule; the current building (1989) sits atop a vast underground concourse linking TRA, HSR, MRT and the Airport MRT — effectively the transport heart of the whole island.

Highlights — What to See
  • The famous underground 'sitting area' where locals picnic on the marble floor
  • Directly connected to Taipei Underground Street for shopping and food
  • Multiple exits leading toward Ximending, Zhongshan and the Presidential Office area

简介与历史

台北车站自1891年清代铁路通车以来一直是台北的交通枢纽，现今的站体建于1989年，整合了台铁、高铁、捷运与机捷，是全台湾最重要的交通核心。

看点推荐

  • 地下大厅著名的"打地铺"席地而坐区
  • 直通台北地下街，购物与美食皆便利
  • 多个出口可通往西门町、中山与总统府周边

Ningxia Night Market宁夏夜市

History & Background

One of Taipei's oldest and most concentrated food-only night markets, tracing its roots to street stalls set up in the Japanese colonial era near Dadaocheng, now celebrated for preserving old-school Taiwanese snack recipes in a compact single street.

Highlights — What to See
  • Braised pork rice (lu rou fan) and oyster omelet stalls with decades of history
  • Milkfish porridge and Taiwanese meatballs (ba-wan)
  • A compact, walkable single-street layout that's easy to explore before returning to the hotel

简介与历史

宁夏夜市是台北历史最悠久、以美食闻名的夜市之一，起源可追溯至日治时期大稻埕一带的路边摊，以保留传统台湾小吃闻名。

看点推荐

  • 卤肉饭、蚵仔煎等老字号摊位
  • 虱目鱼粥、台湾肉圆
  • 街区紧凑，步行即可逛完，方便返回酒店
