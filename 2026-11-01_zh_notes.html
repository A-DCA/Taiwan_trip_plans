Transport Check & Alternatives 交通核实与建议

✓ Transport note: Dihua St ↔ Shilin Residence ↔ Palace Museum ↔ Beitou ↔ Shilin Market ↔ Hotel forms a rough north-south then back-south loop across Taipei's Shilin/Beitou district — the sequencing is sound, but it is a long, transfer-heavy bus day.
⚠ Route check: The 17:00 leg (Palace Museum → Beitou hot spring) at 60 minutes with multiple transfers is the weakest link. A simpler alternative: take Bus 255 or Red 30 from the Museum to Shilin MRT Station (~15 min), then MRT Red Line to Beitou, then the free Xin Beitou shuttle or a 10-minute walk to Beitou Park (total ~35–40 min, one transfer instead of two).
✓ Transport note: Beitou → Shilin Night Market is best done via MRT: Xinbeitou→Beitou→Shilin (Red Line, ~15 min) is faster and simpler than the bus routing shown, and avoids evening traffic on Chengde Rd.
⚠ Route check: Given the museum alone easily takes 2–3 hours and this is already a packed day, consider dropping the Beitou hot-spring soak (or shortening it to a quick foot-bath) if you want to arrive at Shilin Night Market with energy for dinner.

Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Dihua Street迪化街

History & Background

Taipei's oldest surviving commercial street, built up from the 1850s as the hub of the Dadaocheng trading district; its unbroken row of Baroque, Qing- and Japanese-era shophouses once anchored Taiwan's tea, herbal medicine and dry-goods trade with mainland China and beyond.

Highlights — What to See
  • Rows of preserved 19th-century shophouse façades
  • Yongle Market and traditional dried-goods/herbal medicine shops
  • Xiahai City God Temple tucked mid-street, famous for matchmaking blessings

简介与历史

迪化街是台北现存最古老的商业街道，19世纪中叶开始发展，是大稻埕贸易区的核心，曾是台湾茶叶、中药材与南北货贸易的重镇，至今保留大量巴洛克式与闽南式老屋立面。

看点推荐

  • 成排保存完好的19世纪老式店屋立面
  • 永乐市场与传统南北货、中药行
  • 街区中段的霞海城隍庙，以月老姻缘闻名

Chiang Kai-Shek Shilin Residence士林官邸正館

History & Background

Built in 1950 as the official residence of President Chiang Kai-shek and his wife Soong Mei-ling, this compound of gardens and villas served as their home for over 25 years and offers a rare, intimate look at Taiwan's mid-20th-century political history.

Highlights — What to See
  • The Chinese and Western-style gardens, especially the rose garden
  • The original residence building (guided/self-tour areas)
  • Greenhouse and chapel used by the Chiang family

简介与历史

士林官邸建于1950年，是蒋中正与宋美龄的官邸，两人在此居住超过二十五年，园内保留中式与西式庭园，是了解台湾近代政治史的重要场所。

看点推荐

  • 中西合璧庭园，尤以玫瑰园著名
  • 官邸本馆（部分区域开放参观）
  • 家族使用过的温室与教堂

National Palace Museum國立故宮博物院

History & Background

Home to one of the world's largest collections of Chinese imperial art and artifacts, most of it evacuated from Beijing's Forbidden City during the Chinese Civil War and moved to Taiwan in 1948–49; the museum opened at this Taipei site in 1965 and now holds nearly 700,000 pieces spanning 8,000 years.

Highlights — What to See
  • The Jadeite Cabbage and Meat-shaped Stone, two of the museum's most iconic pieces
  • Song- and Qing-dynasty ceramics and calligraphy galleries
  • Bronze ritual vessels dating back to the Shang dynasty

简介与历史

国立故宫博物院收藏了全球数一数二的中国宫廷艺术珍品，多数文物于国共内战期间自北京故宫南迁，1948至49年间辗转运抵台湾，1965年在台北现址开馆，馆藏近70万件，横跨8000年历史。

看点推荐

  • 镇馆之宝翠玉白菜与肉形石
  • 宋代、清代瓷器与书画展厅
  • 商代青铜礼器

Beitou Public Hot Spring北投公園溫泉浴池

History & Background

Beitou's hot springs were developed commercially from 1896 under Japanese rule, making it one of Taiwan's earliest spa resort towns; the sulfurous Thermal Valley waters still feed the public bathhouses and parks that line the Beitou stream today.

Highlights — What to See
  • Beitou Hot Spring Museum, a restored 1913 Japanese-era public bathhouse
  • Thermal Valley (Hell Valley), the steaming spring source
  • Public and private bathing options along Zhongshan Road

简介与历史

北投温泉自1896年日治时期起开发为温泉疗养地，是台湾最早的温泉观光区之一，硫磺泉水至今仍供应沿北投溪而建的公共与民营浴场。

看点推荐

  • 北投温泉博物馆，修复自1913年日治公共浴场
  • 地热谷（地狱谷），温泉水源冒着白烟
  • 中山路沿线的公共与私人浴场

Shilin Night Market士林夜市

History & Background

One of Taipei's largest and most famous night markets, developing since the 1910s around the old Cixian Temple; today it combines a covered food basement with sprawling street stalls, and remains most visitors' first taste of Taiwanese night-market culture.

Highlights — What to See
  • The underground food court for classics like oyster omelet and stinky tofu
  • Street-level clothing, games and snack stalls around Cixian Temple
  • Giant fried chicken cutlet (hashi da ji pai), a market icon

简介与历史

士林夜市是台北规模最大、最知名的夜市之一，自1910年代起在慈諴宫周边发展而成，如今结合地下美食广场与地面摊位，是许多游客体验台湾夜市文化的第一站。

看点推荐

  • 地下美食区：蚵仔煎、臭豆腐等经典小吃
  • 地面摊位：服饰、游戏与小吃环绕慈諴宫
  • 招牌大鸡排
