Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Yehliu GeoPark野柳地质公园

History & Background

A narrow cape on Taiwan's north coast where millions of years of sea erosion, wind and mineral-rich sandstone have sculpted a famous landscape of honeycomb and mushroom rock formations; it has been protected as a scenic area since the 1960s.

Highlights — What to See
  • The Queen's Head rock, the park's most photographed formation
  • Mushroom rocks and honeycomb weathering along the tip of the cape
  • Tide pools and fossil-bearing rock layers visible along the shore trail

简介与历史

野柳地质公园位于台湾北海岸的狭长岬角，历经数百万年海浪侵蚀与矿物沉积作用，形成著名的蜂窝岩与蕈状岩地貌，自1960年代起被列为风景特定区加以保护。

看点推荐

  • 最著名的"女王头"奇石
  • 岬角尖端的蕈状岩与蜂窝岩地形
  • 沿岸步道可见潮池与化石岩层

Shifen Waterfall十分瀑布

History & Background

Nicknamed the 'Little Niagara of Taiwan,' this 20-metre-wide, 20-metre-high curtain waterfall on the Keelung River basin has drawn visitors since the Japanese colonial era built the Pingxi rail line through the valley for coal transport in the 1920s.

Highlights — What to See
  • The wide curtain-style falls, Taiwan's largest waterfall by width
  • Suspension bridge with a head-on viewing platform
  • Surrounding forest trail loop, an easy 20–30 minute walk

简介与历史

十分瀑布素有"台湾尼加拉瀑布"之称，瀑宽约20米、落差约20米，呈帘幕状，自1920年代日治时期修建平溪线铁路运煤以来就是热门景点。

看点推荐

  • 台湾瀑宽最大的帘幕式瀑布
  • 吊桥观景台可正面欣赏瀑布
  • 周边森林环状步道，步行约20–30分钟

Shifen Old Street十分老街

History & Background

A former coal-mining settlement built along the Pingxi rail line in the 1920s–30s; the street is unusual in that the railway tracks run directly through the middle of the shops, a legacy of the line's original freight function.

Highlights — What to See
  • Releasing a sky lantern (tian deng) painted with your wishes, the street's signature activity
  • Shops built right up against the still-active railway tracks
  • Old mining-era shopfronts and snack stalls

简介与历史

十分老街是1920至30年代随平溪线铁路发展而成的采矿聚落，最大特色是铁轨直接穿越街道中央，是当年运煤铁路留下的独特景观。

看点推荐

  • 放天灯，写下愿望，是老街最具代表性的体验
  • 店铺紧贴仍在运行的铁轨而建
  • 保留矿业时期老店面与小吃摊

Houtong Village (Cat Village)猴硐貓村

History & Background

Once a thriving coal-mining town from the 1920s to the 1990s (the ruins of its washery and transfer bridge still stand by the station), Houtong reinvented itself in the 2010s as 'Cat Village' after residents began caring for stray cats around the old mining homes.

Highlights — What to See
  • Hundreds of resident cats roaming cat-themed bridges, cafes and alleyways
  • Remains of the former coal-washing plant and mining structures near the station
  • Houtong Coal Mine Ecological Park exhibits on the area's mining history

简介与历史

猴硐曾是1920至1990年代繁荣的煤矿聚落，车站旁至今保留选煤场与运煤桥遗迹；2010年代因居民照顾流浪猫而转型为知名的"猫村"。

看点推荐

  • 数百只猫咪漫步于猫桥、猫咖啡与巷弄间
  • 车站旁的选煤场与矿业遗迹
  • 猴硐煤矿生态园区，介绍当地采矿历史

Jiufen Old Street九份老街

History & Background

A gold-rush mountain town that boomed during Japanese colonial rule (1895–1945) when it hosted one of Asia's richest gold mines, then fell quiet after the mines closed in 1971 — until a 1989 film revival and its reputed (though debated) links to 'Spirited Away' made it one of Taiwan's most visited old streets.

Highlights — What to See
  • Narrow stepped lanes strung with red lanterns, best at dusk
  • Teahouses built into the hillside with sea views, e.g. the A-Mei Tea House
  • Taiwanese snacks like taro balls (yu yuan) and peanut ice cream rolls

简介与历史

九份在日治时期（1895–1945）因盛产黄金而繁荣一时，曾是亚洲最富饶的金矿区之一；1971年矿业没落后沉寂多年，1989年因电影取景重新走红，也因传闻与《千与千寻》场景相似而闻名国际。

看点推荐

  • 挂满红灯笼的阶梯老街，傍晚最美
  • 依山而建、可眺望海景的茶楼（如阿妹茶楼）
  • 芋圆、花生卷冰淇淋等台式小吃
