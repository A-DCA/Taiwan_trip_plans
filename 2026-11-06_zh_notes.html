Transport Check & Alternatives 交通核实与建议

✓ Transport note: The sunrise-train logistics are correctly planned: the Chushan Line departs before dawn (typically 05:00–06:00 depending on season, matching your 05:55 slot for early November) and the round trip plus viewing time fits comfortably into the 05:35–07:00 window shown.
⚠ Route check: There is a large, unexplained gap between 09:10 (leaving Alishan Bus Station) and 15:30 (arriving Chiayi Station) — about 6 hours for what the note describes as a 150–180 minute bus ride. Either the bus departs later than 09:10 (e.g. midday), or there is several hours of free time built in at Chiayi city itself; please confirm the actual 7322(C) departure time so the schedule reflects reality — as written it reads as a gap rather than a single transfer.
✓ Transport note: If the extra hours are intentional free time, Chiayi City is worth 2–3 hours: the Hinoki Village (a preserved Japanese-era timber-worker village) and Chiayi Confucius Temple are both walkable from the TRA station and make good use of a layover.
✓ Transport note: Chiayi → Kaohsiung by TRA (regular rail, not THSR) at ~90 minutes is realistic only on a Tze-Chiang Limited Express (the fastest TRA service); regular Chu-Kuang or local trains take noticeably longer (2–2.5 hrs), so double-check you're booked on the express service, matching the plan's 'booking in advance required' note.
✓ Transport note: An alternative for this leg, if TRA tickets are unavailable, is THSR from Chiayi HSR Station (not the in-town TRA station, so allow extra transfer time) to Zuoying Station, which takes only ~35–40 minutes but requires an extra local transfer at both ends.

Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Chushan Sunrise Viewing Platform祝山觀日平台

History & Background

At 2,451m, Chushan is the terminus of Alishan Forest Railway's highest branch line, built specifically to bring visitors up for the sunrise; the platform has been Taiwan's most famous sunrise-viewing spot since the railway extension opened in the mid-20th century, prized for views over the Yushan mountain range.

Highlights — What to See
  • Sunrise breaking over the Central Mountain Range, with Yushan (Taiwan's tallest peak) often visible
  • The chance of a 'sea of clouds' filling the valleys below on clear mornings
  • The historic narrow-gauge sunrise train itself, one of the last such rides in Asia

简介与历史

祝山海拔2451米，是阿里山森林铁路海拔最高的支线终点，专为接送旅客上山看日出而建，自20世纪中叶通车以来一直是台湾最负盛名的日出观赏地，可眺望玉山山脉。

看点推荐

  • 旭日自中央山脉升起，天气好时可远眺台湾最高峰玉山
  • 晴朗清晨山谷间可能出现壮观的云海
  • 搭乘历史悠久的高山观日小火车，是亚洲仅存的同类体验之一

Liouhe Night Market六合國際觀光夜市

History & Background

One of Kaohsiung's oldest and most internationally known night markets, developed from roadside stalls in the 1950s along Liuhe 2nd Road near the TRA station; it became a flagship 'tourist night market' in the 1980s as Kaohsiung promoted itself to international visitors.

Highlights — What to See
  • A dense strip of seafood stalls, from oyster omelets to giant shrimp
  • Papaya milk, a Kaohsiung specialty drink said to have originated near this market
  • Easy walking distance from Kaohsiung Main Station and many hotels

简介与历史

六合夜市是高雄历史最悠久、国际知名度最高的夜市之一，1950年代由六合二路的路边摊发展而成，1980年代高雄推广国际观光时成为代表性的"观光夜市"。

看点推荐

  • 海鲜摊位林立，从蚵仔煎到大明虾应有尽有
  • 高雄特色饮品木瓜牛奶，相传起源于此夜市附近
  • 距离高雄车站与多数酒店都在步行范围内
