Transport Check & Alternatives 交通核实与建议

✓ Transport note: Kaohsiung MRT's Red Line runs directly to Kaohsiung International Airport Station, and the ride from Kaohsiung Main Station is genuinely about 25–28 minutes with trains roughly every 8–10 minutes — this is the simplest and most reliable option, no transfer needed.
✓ Transport note: For an international departure, arriving at the airport ~2.5–3 hours before a flight is standard; an 08:30 airport arrival works well for a flight departing around 11:00–11:30. If your flight is later, no need to rush — Kaohsiung Airport is compact and check-in is usually quick.
✓ Transport note: A taxi from most downtown Kaohsiung hotels to the airport costs roughly NT$300–400 and takes about 15–20 minutes — a good backup if you have heavy luggage or an earlier flight.

Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Kaohsiung International Airport高雄國際機場

History & Background

Originally a Japanese military airfield built in the 1940s, Kaohsiung's airport was converted to civilian use after WWII and has grown into southern Taiwan's main international gateway, connecting Kaohsiung directly to destinations across East and Southeast Asia and mainland China.

Highlights — What to See
  • Direct MRT access right into the terminal, unusual convenience for an international airport
  • A compact, easy-to-navigate single-building layout
  • Last chance for Taiwanese snacks and souvenirs before departure

简介与历史

高雄国际机场原为1940年代日治时期修建的军用机场，二战后转为民用，如今已发展为台湾南部最主要的国际门户机场，直飞航线覆盖东亚、东南亚及中国大陆多个城市。

看点推荐

  • 捷运可直达航站楼，国际机场中少见的便利
  • 航站规模紧凑，动线简单好找路
  • 登机前最后购买台湾伴手礼与小吃的机会
