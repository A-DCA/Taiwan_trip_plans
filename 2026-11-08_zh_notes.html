Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Kaohsiung International Airport高雄國際機場

History & Background

Originally a Japanese military airfield built in the 1940s, Kaohsiung's airport was converted to civilian use after WWII and has grown into southern Taiwan's main international gateway.

Highlights — What to See
  • Direct MRT access right into the terminal
  • A compact, easy-to-navigate single-building layout
  • Last chance for Taiwanese snacks and souvenirs before departure

简介与历史

高雄国际机场原为1940年代日治时期修建的军用机场，二战后转为民用，如今已发展为台湾南部最主要的国际门户机场。

看点推荐

  • 捷运可直达航站楼，十分便利
  • 航站规模紧凑，动线简单
  • 登机前最后购买台湾伴手礼的机会

Nanjing Lukou International Airport南京禄口國際機場

History & Background

Opened in 1997 to replace Nanjing's older Dajiaochang airport, Lukou serves as Jiangsu province's main international gateway and a common transfer point between Taiwan/Southeast Asia routes and inland Chinese cities.

Highlights — What to See
  • Straightforward domestic transfer facilities for onward flights
  • Metro S1 line connection to central Nanjing, if you ever have a longer layover
  • A good rest stop if your connection allows a short break

简介与历史

南京禄口国际机场于1997年启用，取代了原先的南京大校场机场，是江苏省最主要的国际门户机场，也是台湾/东南亚航线与中国内陆城市之间常见的中转点。

看点推荐

  • 设施完善的国内转机流程
  • 地铁S1号线连接南京市区（若转机时间较长可考虑）
  • 转机间隙可稍作休息的良好选择

Xi'an Xianyang International Airport西安咸陽國際機場

History & Background

Opened in 1991 northwest of the city in Xianyang, this is the main air gateway to Xi'an and the wider Shaanxi region — the historic heartland of ancient Chang'an, capital of thirteen Chinese dynasties including the Qin, Han and Tang.

Highlights — What to See
  • Metro Line 14 direct connection into central Xi'an, opened in 2021
  • A first glimpse of Shaanxi's blend of ancient history and modern development
  • Onward connections to nearly all major domestic Chinese cities

简介与历史

西安咸阳国际机场于1991年在西安西北方向的咸阳启用，是西安及陕西地区的主要空中门户——这里正是古都长安的所在地，曾是秦、汉、唐等十三朝古都。

看点推荐

  • 2021年开通的地铁14号线可直达市区
  • 初步领略陕西古今交融的城市风貌
  • 可转乘航班前往中国几乎所有主要城市
