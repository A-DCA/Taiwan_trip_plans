Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Shaanxi History Museum陝西歷史博物館

History & Background

Opened in 1991 and expanded in the 2020s, this is one of China's first large modern museums and the premier repository of relics from Shaanxi's 13 dynastic capitals — including the Zhou, Qin, Han and Tang — holding over 370,000 artifacts, from Zhou bronzes to Tang gold and silverware.

Highlights — What to See
  • Tang-dynasty gilded silverware and mural fragments from imperial tombs
  • Han and Tang dynasty pottery figurines, precursors to the terracotta warrior tradition
  • Zhou-dynasty bronze ritual vessels inscribed with early Chinese script

简介与历史

陕西历史博物馆开馆于1991年，是中国最早的大型现代化博物馆之一，收藏陕西13朝古都出土文物逾37万件，涵盖周、秦、汉、唐等各代，堪称了解陕西乃至中国古代文明的窗口。

看点推荐

  • 唐代金银器与墓葬壁画残片
  • 汉唐陶俑，兵马俑传统的先声
  • 刻有早期文字的周代青铜礼器

Giant Wild Goose Pagoda & Da Ci'en Temple大雁塔與大慈恩寺

History & Background

Built in 652 CE under Emperor Gaozong of Tang to house Buddhist sutras and statues brought back from India by the monk Xuanzang (the real-life basis for 'Journey to the West'), this 64-metre brick pagoda is one of China's oldest and best-preserved Tang-era structures, still standing within the working Da Ci'en Temple.

Highlights — What to See
  • The pagoda's original Tang-dynasty brick construction, climbable for city views
  • Stone tablets inside recording Xuanzang's pilgrimage and translation work
  • Da Ci'en Temple's halls, an active place of Buddhist worship since 648 CE

简介与历史

大雁塔建于唐永徽三年（652年），由唐高宗为存放高僧玄奘自印度带回的佛经佛像而建，塔高64米，是中国现存最古老、保存最完好的唐代建筑之一，至今仍矗立于仍在使用的大慈恩寺内。

看点推荐

  • 塔身保留唐代原始砖构，可登塔俯瞰西安城景
  • 塔内石碑记载玄奘西行取经与译经事迹
  • 大慈恩寺自648年起持续香火，至今仍是佛教活动场所

Great Tang All-Day Mall (Tang Everbright City)大唐不夜城

History & Background

A modern (2019-era) pedestrian district built directly south of the Wild Goose Pagoda, designed to recreate the atmosphere of Tang-dynasty Chang'an through Tang-style architecture, statuary and nightly light shows — now one of China's most-visited urban night attractions.

Highlights — What to See
  • Larger-than-life bronze statues of Tang-dynasty poets and scenes, popular photo spots
  • Elaborate nightly light and projection shows on the main boulevard
  • Street performers in Tang-style costume recreating historical vignettes

简介与历史

大唐不夜城是位于大雁塔正南方的现代步行街区（约2019年建成），以唐代长安城风貌为设计灵感，融合仿唐建筑、雕塑与夜间灯光秀，是中国最受欢迎的城市夜游景点之一。

看点推荐

  • 真人比例的唐代诗人与场景铜雕，热门拍照点
  • 主街道每晚的灯光投影表演
  • 身着唐装的街头表演者重现历史场景

Furong Xi Lu芙蓉西路

History & Background

A dining and nightlife street bordering Tang Paradise (Furongyuan) park in the Qujiang district, developed alongside Xi'an's 2000s-era tourism redevelopment of the area, popular with both locals and visitors for its dense concentration of restaurants and street food after a day of sightseeing.

Highlights — What to See
  • A relaxed, mostly local dining strip away from the busier tourist crowds
  • Proximity to Tang Paradise park's illuminated gardens at night
  • A good spot to try Shaanxi comfort food like roujiamo and biangbiang noodles

简介与历史

芙蓉西路是曲江新区内毗邻大唐芙蓉园的餐饮与夜生活街区，随2000年代西安旅游开发而兴起，因餐馆与小吃摊密集，深受本地人与游客欢迎，是一天游览后放松用餐的好去处。

看点推荐

  • 相对悠闲、少游客的本地餐饮街区
  • 夜晚可顺道欣赏大唐芙蓉园的灯光夜景
  • 适合品尝肉夹馍、biangbiang面等陕西家常美食
