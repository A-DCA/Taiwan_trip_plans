Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Terracotta Army (Mausoleum of Emperor Qin Shi Huang)秦始皇帝陵博物院（兵馬俑）

History & Background

Discovered by farmers digging a well in 1974, this vast underground army of an estimated 8,000 life-sized terracotta soldiers, horses and chariots was buried around 210 BCE to guard the tomb of Qin Shi Huang, China's first emperor and unifier — it is one of the most significant archaeological finds of the 20th century and a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1987.

Highlights — What to See
  • Pit 1, the largest excavation hall, with rows of soldiers in battle formation
  • Individually sculpted faces — no two warriors are exactly alike
  • Bronze chariots and horses displayed in the exhibition hall

简介与历史

兵马俑于1974年由农民打井时意外发现，是约公元前210年为守护中国首位皇帝、统一六国的秦始皇陵墓而建的地下军团，估计有8000余尊真人比例的陶俑、陶马与战车，是20世纪最重大的考古发现之一，1987年列入联合国教科文组织世界遗产。

看点推荐

  • 一号坑，规模最大的展坑，可见排列成阵的兵俑
  • 每尊兵俑面部雕刻各异，无一雷同
  • 展览馆内陈列的青铜车马

Huaqing Palace華清宮

History & Background

A royal hot-spring retreat used by Chinese emperors since the Zhou dynasty, rebuilt to its most famous form under Tang Emperor Xuanzong in the 8th century as a pleasure palace for his beloved consort Yang Guifei; it is also where Chiang Kai-shek was briefly detained during the 1936 Xi'an Incident, a pivotal moment in modern Chinese history.

Highlights — What to See
  • The Nine Dragon Lake and Tang-style pavilions built over natural hot springs
  • Lotus Pool, Yang Guifei's legendary bathing pool
  • The site of the 1936 Xi'an Incident, where Chiang Kai-shek was held

简介与历史

华清宫是历代帝王使用的皇家温泉行宫，最早可追溯至周代，8世纪唐玄宗为宠妃杨贵妃扩建成著名的华清宫；1936年"西安事变"期间，蒋介石也曾在此被扣押，是近代中国历史的重要转折点。

看点推荐

  • 九龙湖与依温泉而建的唐式亭台楼阁
  • 相传为杨贵妃沐浴之处的莲花汤遗址
  • 1936年西安事变蒋介石被扣押的历史现场

Bell Tower & Drum Tower鐘樓與鼓樓

History & Background

Built in 1384 during the early Ming dynasty at the exact geographic center of walled Xi'an, the Bell and Drum Towers once regulated the city's daily rhythm — the bell rang at dawn, the drum sounded at dusk — and today anchor the city's night market and Muslim Quarter food streets.

Highlights — What to See
  • The Bell Tower's Ming-dynasty wooden structure, illuminated at night
  • The Drum Tower, gateway to the Muslim Quarter's famous food street
  • Nearby snack alleys serving Xi'an specialties like yangrou paomo and biangbiang noodles

简介与历史

钟楼与鼓楼建于明洪武十七年（1384年），位于西安古城正中心，昔日"晨钟暮鼓"报时，钟楼报晓、鼓楼报暮，如今是回民街美食街区与夜市的地标入口。

看点推荐

  • 夜晚灯火通明的明代钟楼木构建筑
  • 鼓楼，通往著名回民街美食街的门户
  • 周边小吃巷弄，可品尝羊肉泡馍、biangbiang面等西安特色
