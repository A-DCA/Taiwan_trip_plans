Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Xi'an City Wall西安城牆

History & Background

Built in 1370–78 under the first Ming emperor atop earlier Tang-dynasty foundations, Xi'an's city wall is the most complete surviving ancient city wall in China — a 13.7 km rectangular circuit with a moat, watchtowers and gates.

Highlights — What to See
  • Continuous ramparts wide enough to walk or cycle along the full loop
  • Watchtowers and gate complexes at each cardinal entrance
  • Views down into both the old city and the surrounding moat park

简介与历史

西安城墙始建于明洪武三年至十一年（1370–1378年），在唐代皇城基础上扩建而成，是中国现存最完整的古代城垣，周长13.7公里。

看点推荐

  • 宽阔的城墙顶部，可步行或骑行环绕全程
  • 各主要城门处的箭楼与瓮城建筑
  • 俯瞰城内老城区与护城河公园景色

Forest of Stone Steles Museum碑林博物館

History & Background

Founded in 1087 during the Northern Song dynasty to preserve Tang-dynasty stone Confucian classics, this is China's largest collection of ancient stone steles and calligraphy, housed in a former Confucian temple.

Highlights — What to See
  • The Kaicheng Stone Classics, twelve Confucian texts carved in stone during the Tang dynasty
  • Calligraphy steles by China's most celebrated calligraphers
  • The Nestorian Stele, an 8th-century record of early Christianity in China

简介与历史

碑林博物馆创建于北宋元祐二年（1087年），是中国规模最大的古代碑石与书法收藏地，馆址原为孔庙与官学。

看点推荐

  • 开成石经，唐代刻于石碑上的十二部儒家经典
  • 历代书法大家碑刻
  • 大秦景教流行中国碑，记录早期基督教传入中国的史料

Yongxingfang永興坊

History & Background

A recreated Tang-style food street opened in 2015 on the site of a historic Tang-dynasty government quarter, famous nationally for its theatrical 'smashed' liquor-soaked bread (mo) stall.

Highlights — What to See
  • The liquor-soaked mo (bread) stall, smashed and set alight tableside
  • A dense concentration of Shaanxi snack stalls under one roof
  • Tang-style architecture evoking the historic quarter

简介与历史

永兴坊是2015年在唐代永兴坊旧址上重建的仿唐美食街区，因"摔碗酒"泡馍摊位的戏剧化表演而爆红全国。

看点推荐

  • "摔碗酒"泡馍摊位，现场摔碗、点火表演
  • 陕西各式小吃摊位集中一处
  • 仿唐建筑风格

Xi'an Xianyang International Airport西安咸陽國際機場

History & Background

Xi'an's main air gateway, opened in 1991 northwest of the city, connecting the historic Shaanxi region to the rest of China and the world.

Highlights — What to See
  • Metro Line 14 direct connection into central Xi'an
  • A convenient departure point for onward domestic flights like this evening's Shanghai connection

简介与历史

西安咸阳国际机场1991年启用，是西安及陕西地区的主要空中门户，连接全中国及世界各地。

看点推荐

  • 地铁14号线可直达市区
  • 便利的国内航班转机点，如本次飞往上海的航班
