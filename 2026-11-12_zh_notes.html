Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Shanghai Tower上海中心大廈

History & Background

Completed in 2015, this 632-metre twisting skyscraper in Lujiazui is the tallest building in China and the world's second-tallest, distinguished by its spiraling glass facade engineered to reduce wind load by 24% — a design as much about structural efficiency as spectacle.

Highlights — What to See
  • The observation deck near the top, among the highest in the world
  • The twisting double-skin facade, visible from the atrium floors
  • Cloud Bookstore (云朵书店), a striking multi-level bookshop built into the tower's base

简介与历史

上海中心大厦于2015年建成，位于陆家嘴，塔高632米，是中国第一高楼、世界第二高楼，其螺旋形玻璃幕墙设计可降低约24%的风荷载，兼具结构效率与视觉震撼。

看点推荐

  • 位居全球最高之列的观光层
  • 可从中庭楼层欣赏的双层旋转幕墙结构
  • 塔底别具一格的多层书店"云朵书店"

Yu Garden豫園

History & Background

A classical Jiangnan-style private garden built in 1559 during the Ming dynasty by a government official for his aging parents, Yu Garden (meaning 'Garden of Peace and Comfort') took nearly 20 years to complete and remains one of the finest surviving examples of Ming-era landscape design in China.

Highlights — What to See
  • Intricate rockeries, ponds and pavilions arranged in classical Suzhou-garden style
  • The Exquisite Jade Rock, a famous Taihu limestone centerpiece
  • Zigzag bridges and dragon-topped walls dividing the garden into scenic 'rooms'

简介与历史

豫园始建于明嘉靖三十八年（1559年），由官员潘允端为侍奉年迈父母而建，历时近20年建成，"豫"取"平安、安泰"之意，是中国现存最精美的明代江南园林之一。

看点推荐

  • 精巧的假山、池塘与亭台，典型苏州园林风格
  • "玉玲珑"太湖石，园中著名地标
  • 曲桥与龙墙，将园林分隔成多个景致"房间"

City God Temple (Chenghuang Miao)城隍廟

History & Background

A Taoist temple complex dating to the Ming dynasty (1403–24), dedicated to Shanghai's city guardian deities; though much of the surrounding area was rebuilt in the 20th century as a commercial bazaar, the temple itself remains an active place of worship at the historic heart of Old Shanghai.

Highlights — What to See
  • The main temple halls housing the city god statues, still an active worship site
  • The surrounding Yuyuan Bazaar's maze of shops selling snacks, tea and souvenirs
  • Traditional snack stalls serving Shanghai specialties like xiaolongbao

简介与历史

城隍庙始建于明永乐年间（1403–1424年），供奉上海城隍神，虽周边商业街区于20世纪重建为集市，庙宇本身至今仍是老上海核心地带的活跃宗教场所。

看点推荐

  • 供奉城隍神的主殿，至今香火不断
  • 周边豫园商城，店铺林立，售卖小吃、茶叶与纪念品
  • 传统小吃摊，可品尝南翔小笼包等上海特色

The Bund外灘

History & Background

A 1.5 km waterfront promenade along the Huangpu River, lined with over 50 buildings in Beaux-Arts, Gothic and Art Deco styles built by foreign banks and trading houses between the 1860s and 1930s, when Shanghai's International Settlement made it a major global financial and trading hub.

Highlights — What to See
  • The historic 'Bund skyline' of 1920s–30s colonial-era buildings on the west bank
  • Panoramic views across the river to Lujiazui's futuristic towers (Shanghai Tower, Jin Mao, SWFC) on the east bank
  • Evening building illumination, best viewed after dusk

简介与历史

外滩是沿黄浦江而建的1.5公里滨江步道，19世纪60年代至20世纪30年代间，外国银行与洋行在此建造了逾50栋新古典、哥特与装饰艺术风格建筑，见证上海作为国际租界时期全球金融贸易枢纽的历史。

看点推荐

  • 西岸保留完好的1920–30年代殖民时期"外滩天际线"
  • 隔江可远眺陆家嘴未来感十足的摩天楼群（上海中心、金茂大厦、环球金融中心）
  • 入夜后的建筑灯光秀，日落后最为壮观

Nanjing Road Pedestrian Street南京路步行街

History & Background

China's first and busiest shopping street, developed from the 1850s as Shanghai's main commercial thoroughfare under foreign concession rule; the pedestrianized central section has been car-free since 1999 and remains the city's most visited retail destination.

Highlights — What to See
  • A 1.2 km fully pedestrianized shopping strip with department stores old and new
  • Historic Republican-era shopfronts alongside modern retail
  • The small sightseeing tram running the length of the pedestrian section

简介与历史

南京路自1850年代起在租界时期发展为上海的主要商业街，是中国最早、人流最密集的商业街，核心步行段自1999年起禁止车辆通行，至今仍是上海人气最旺的购物地标。

看点推荐

  • 1.2公里全步行购物街，新旧百货云集
  • 民国时期老店面与现代商铺并存
  • 贯穿步行街的观光小火车
