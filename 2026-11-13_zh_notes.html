Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Zhongshuge Bookstore鐘書書店

History & Background

Part of a chain of striking bookstores designed by Shanghai-based X+Living studio since the 2010s, this location is known for its mirrored ceilings and undulating black bookshelves that create the illusion of an infinite, cave-like library — as much an architectural attraction as a place to browse books.

Highlights — What to See
  • Mirrored ceiling creating an illusion of endless bookshelves
  • Sculptural, wave-like shelving design
  • A quieter, design-focused counterpoint to Shanghai's busier tourist sites

简介与历史

钟书阁由上海本土设计事务所X+Living自2010年代起设计打造，以镜面天花板与波浪状黑色书架闻名，营造出仿佛无限延伸的洞穴式书店效果，兼具建筑美学与阅读空间双重魅力。

看点推荐

  • 镜面天花板营造无限书架的视觉效果
  • 雕塑感十足的波浪形书架设计
  • 相对安静、注重设计美学的景点，有别于繁忙的游客热点

Jing'an Temple靜安寺

History & Background

One of Shanghai's oldest Buddhist temples, traditionally dated to 247 CE and relocated to its current site in 1216 during the Southern Song dynasty; the current gold-roofed halls date largely from a 2010s reconstruction, creating a striking contrast between ancient Buddhist worship and the surrounding modern skyscrapers.

Highlights — What to See
  • Gilded roofs and a towering main hall rebuilt in Song-dynasty style
  • A large bronze incense burner where worshippers make offerings
  • The dramatic juxtaposition of the temple against Jing'an's modern high-rises

简介与历史

静安寺是上海历史最悠久的佛教寺院之一，相传始建于三国吴赤乌十年（247年），南宋嘉定年间（1216年）迁至现址；如今金顶殿宇多为2010年代重建，与周边现代摩天楼形成鲜明对比。

看点推荐

  • 仿宋式重建的金顶大殿
  • 供信众上香祭拜的大型铜香炉
  • 古寺与静安现代高楼交相辉映的强烈反差

Wukang Road & Wukang Building武康路與武康大樓

History & Background

Wukang Road, laid out in the 1900s–20s as part of the French Concession, is lined with historic garden villas once home to writers, diplomats and film stars; at its northern end stands the 1924 Normandie Apartments (Wukang Building), a flatiron-shaped Renaissance-style building that has become one of Shanghai's most photographed pieces of architecture.

Highlights — What to See
  • The triangular, ship-prow-shaped Wukang Building at the road's junction
  • Tree-lined streets of 1920s–30s garden villas along Wukang Road
  • Small independent cafes and boutiques occupying former Concession-era houses

简介与历史

武康路是1900至1920年代法租界时期规划的街道，两旁多为曾住过作家、外交官与电影明星的花园洋房；北端矗立着1924年建成的武康大楼（原诺曼底公寓），因独特的船首造型三角建筑而成为上海最具标志性、最常被拍摄的建筑之一。

看点推荐

  • 路口船首造型的三角形武康大楼
  • 武康路沿线1920–30年代花园洋房
  • 租界时期老洋房改造的独立咖啡馆与精品小店

Site of the 1st CPC National Congress & Xintiandi一大會址與新天地

History & Background

The site where the Chinese Communist Party held its founding congress in July 1921, preserved within a traditional shikumen ('stone-gate') lane house; the surrounding Xintiandi district was redeveloped from 2001 onward, restoring rows of similar shikumen buildings as an upscale dining, shopping and nightlife quarter.

Highlights — What to See
  • The preserved shikumen building where the 1921 founding congress was held, now a museum
  • Xintiandi's restored shikumen lane-house architecture, a distinctive Shanghai building style
  • A dense concentration of restaurants, bars and boutiques for an evening out

简介与历史

中共一大会址是1921年7月中国共产党第一次全国代表大会的召开地，保存于典型的石库门里弄建筑内；周边新天地街区自2001年起重新开发，修复大片同类石库门建筑，打造成高端餐饮、购物与夜生活街区。

看点推荐

  • 保存完好的石库门建筑，1921年一大召开地，现为纪念馆
  • 新天地修复的石库门里弄建筑群，上海独特的建筑风格
  • 密集的餐厅、酒吧与精品店，适合夜间休闲

Shanghai Circus World上海馬戲城

History & Background

Purpose-built in 1999 as a dedicated home for large-scale acrobatic and circus performance, this venue has since 2005 hosted 'ERA – Intersection of Time,' a long-running acrobatics spectacular blending traditional Chinese circus skills with modern staging, motorcycles and aerial artistry — one of Shanghai's longest-running theatrical productions.

Highlights — What to See
  • 'ERA – Intersection of Time,' the resident acrobatics and motorcycle show
  • Traditional Chinese acrobatic disciplines like pole-climbing, contortion and plate-spinning
  • A purpose-built theater designed specifically for large-scale circus staging

简介与历史

上海马戏城于1999年专为大型杂技与马戏表演而建，自2005年起常年上演王牌节目《时空之旅》，将中国传统杂技技艺与现代舞台、摩托车特技及空中表演相结合，是上海演出时间最长的驻场演出之一。

看点推荐

  • 常年驻演的《时空之旅》杂技摩托车秀
  • 爬杆、柔术、转碟等中国传统杂技项目
  • 专为大型杂技演出设计建造的专业剧场
