Places — History & Highlights 景点介绍与看点

Zhujiajiao Water Town朱家角

History & Background

One of the best-preserved of the 'Jiangnan water towns' near Shanghai, Zhujiajiao's canal-side settlement dates back over 1,700 years, with most of its surviving stone bridges, bluestone lanes and waterside houses built during the Ming and Qing dynasties, when it flourished as a rice and silk trading post connected by boat to the wider Yangtze delta.

Highlights — What to See
  • Fangsheng Bridge, a five-arch Ming-dynasty stone bridge (1571) spanning the main canal, the town's best-known landmark
  • Canal-side lanes lined with Ming/Qing-era shophouses, now housing tea houses, snack stalls and craft shops
  • Short sightseeing boat rides along the canal, offering a classic water-town photo angle

简介与历史

朱家角是上海周边保存最完好的江南水乡古镇之一，聚落历史可追溯至1700多年前，现存大部分石桥、石板巷弄与临水民居建于明清两代，当年因水运发达，是连接长江三角洲的重要稻米与丝绸贸易集散地。

看点推荐

  • 放生桥，建于明隆庆五年（1571年）的五孔石拱桥，横跨主河道，是古镇最著名地标
  • 沿河明清风格老街，如今遍布茶馆、小吃摊与手工艺品店
  • 短程摇橹船游河，是拍摄水乡风情的经典角度
