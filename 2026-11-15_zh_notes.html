Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport上海虹橋國際機場

History & Background

Shanghai's original airport, opened in 1923 and long serving as the city's main civil aviation gateway before Pudong International Airport took over most long-haul international traffic in 1999; today Hongqiao focuses on domestic and short-haul regional flights, and sits directly beside Hongqiao Railway Station, one of China's busiest high-speed rail hubs.

Highlights — What to See
  • Two well-connected terminals (T1 for most airlines, T2 handling the bulk of scheduled domestic/international carriers)
  • Direct integration with Hongqiao Railway Station for onward high-speed rail across the Yangtze Delta
  • A useful last stop for Shanghai souvenirs or a final meal before departure

简介与历史

上海虹桥国际机场于1923年启用，曾长期作为上海主要的民用航空门户，直至1999年浦东国际机场启用后承接大部分远程国际航线；如今虹桥机场主要负责国内及区域短途航班，并与虹桥火车站（中国最繁忙的高铁枢纽之一）紧密相连。

看点推荐

  • 两座航站楼（T1主要服务部分航空公司，T2承担大部分国内国际定期航班）衔接便利
  • 与虹桥火车站无缝连接，可换乘高铁前往长三角各地
  • 登机前购买上海伴手礼或用餐的最后机会
